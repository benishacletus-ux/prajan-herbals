About Prajan Herbals

Natural herbal juices for healthy living — thoughtfully crafted from Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu.

Wellness made simple

Prajan Herbals is a family-run herbal juice brand from Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu. We craft our juices inspired by traditional Ayurvedic wellness, using natural ingredients and a quality-first approach that has been part of our family for generations.

Our range includes G2L, Amla Juice and Noni Juice — each made in small, fresh batches so you get the full goodness of nature in every bottle.

Whether you're starting your wellness journey or looking for a trusted daily companion, we're here to make natural living effortless.
